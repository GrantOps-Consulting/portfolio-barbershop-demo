— Trade —

A Quiet Work Continues in the Trongate

The Barber Co. enters its eighth year of trade, with six chairs operating daily and the same unfashionable principle: every cut deserves the time it requires.

The Trongate Barber Co., established at the foot of the Tron eight years past, continues quietly to serve the men of Glasgow's east end with the care of an older trade. Six chairs operate daily; bookings are taken by telegraph; walk-ins received with civility.

The shop, under the direction of L. Carrigan and a roster of four floor-coaches, has built its custom on the unfashionable principle that every cut deserves the time it requires. No appointment is hurried, no client rebuffed at the door for want of a booking — the chair is held, where space permits, against the next available barber.

Members of the ladies' auxiliary occasionally enquire after our hours; we welcome all who would sit in the chair, and the rates are advertised in the menu adjacent. The premises are open six days, with Sunday by telegraphed appointment only — a small concession to those who must shave on a weekend.

— The Booking Office —

A Telegraph
to the Chair.

Bookings are received by Nearcut, the system used in-shop. The wires receive your message instantly; the chair is held the moment the office confirms.

For more than a century the Trongate has done its business by telegraph; we continue the practice, in a modern hand. Every booking made through the office below appears on the same calendar our barbers consult before the first cut of the morning — there is no second-hand list, no message held in the post.

Should the wire be down, walk in. Should the chair be full, leave your name. Should the day be Sunday, send word the night before — we shall meet you at the door if your business requires it.
