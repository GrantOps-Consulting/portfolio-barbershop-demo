The Shop ✦

The chair, since 2018.

Eight years at the foot of the Tron. Six chairs. Six barbers. One philosophy: every cut gets the time the cut needs — no rush at the back of the queue, no cut shortened to make a slot.

We trade walk-ins fast and bookings to the dot, but never the finish for either. Skin fades, hot-towel shaves, beard work, gentleman's cuts — done with the patience of an older trade and the standards of a newer one.
